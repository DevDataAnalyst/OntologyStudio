# HR data dictionary (sample business doc for the enrichment step)

- **Employee**: anyone on payroll; also called staff, worker, headcount.
- **Grade**: pay band. A = Senior, B = Mid-level, C = Junior.
- **Region** of a department: East, West or North sales region.
- **Headcount** (KPI): number of distinct employees.
- **Average salary** (KPI): mean salary of employees in scope.
- Rule: "current staff" means employees with a hire date (hired_on is not null).
